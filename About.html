The Creators And Our Story

Hey My Name is Nitro, I Started Composite Drive Games With My Friend Wyatt Because I Wanted A Place Where I Could Play Unblocked Games And Without There Being A Million Ads Being Shown To Me While Im Playing Games, I Started Making The Site On March 25th 2025 And It Has Come A Long Way Since Then.

What Is Composite Drive Games:

In Our School, We Used to Have A Collaborative Drive On All The School Computers Called The "Composite" Which Everyone Called It "The Composite Drive". Everyone Including Me and Wyatt Used To Put Games, Movies, Cool Batch Files/Trolls, Apps, Music And Funny Images Every Now And Then. This Had Lasted Even Before Me And Wyatt Started HighSchool, Unfortunetly In 2023 and 2024 The Drive Stared To Die Because Random People Would Delete Everything On There Including Some Of The Teachers Being Mainly Our IT Guy. Everyone At Our School Now Uses Unblocked Games Sites And Have Been For Roughly 18 Months. Me And Wyatt Had An Idea To Bring Back The Glory Of "The Composite Drive". We Decided To Make A Google Sites (Which Is Still Available) And Brand It As "Composite Drive Games" Also Known As "CD Games". Unfortuntely The Site Started To Die So Me And Wyatt Decided To Make A Github Site Becuase We Wanted Our Site To Look Unique And Original.

What Me & Wyatt Plan To Do In The Future

We Will Continue To Update The Site And Keep Ads Minimal, I Will Never Put Ads In Any Of Our Games.


We Plan To Implement Movies, Cool Things, Music, ChatRooms And Unblockers/Proxys


We Might Make Ads For Our Youtube Channels, Sponsored Games, Reviews, The Ability To Request Games And Much More To Come To Provide You With The Information You Might Need
